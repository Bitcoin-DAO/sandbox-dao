Sandbox DAO.

A Bitcoin DAO you can join, run and close over a few weeks, using test coins with no value. The point is to learn how a group can make decisions and hold a shared treasury on Bitcoin, by doing it together.

What this is

A showcase for Bitcoin DAOs

A DAO here is a coordination and treasury tool. A group shares a Bitcoin wallet that no single person controls, makes proposals, votes on them, and moves money only when enough keyholders sign. As Bitcoin is a permissionless alternative to money, a Bitcoin DAO is a permissionless alternative to a registered company, co-op or association.

The Sandbox DAO runs on btc-dao, open-source software used through a simple website. It uses the same rules a real DAO would, on Bitcoin's test network, and everyone takes part remotely from their phone.

Two kinds of member

Voters and keyholders

Voting members

Anyone from Club Orange who joins.

  • Make proposals
  • Vote yes, no or abstain within three days
  • Discuss in the BTC DAO group

Council

3-of-5
  • Five members each hold one key to the treasury
  • Any three must sign before coins move
  • Sign only what the members voted for

Everyone signs in with a passkey on their own phone. Council members also get a test treasury key, created on their phone when they sign up, and sign payments there. No hardware device is needed for the practice; a real DAO on mainnet uses dedicated signing devices.

The exercises

Eight steps, start to finish

The practice runs as a sequence. Each step uses a real feature of the app, so by the end every member has used every part of the DAO.

  1. 1

    Found the DAO

    founding

    Five council volunteers and the first members sign up on the website and post their short codes in the group. Three council approvals bring the DAO into being.

    Founders · council approves
  2. 2

    Fund the treasury

    deposit

    Test coins arrive from the BTC DAO Testnet Treasury. Watch them appear on the treasury page.

    Everyone watches
  3. 3

    Welcome a member

    add member

    A new person signs up, posts their short code in the group, and is proposed and voted in.

    Everyone votes
  4. 4

    Pay a member

    payout

    A member proposes a payment to their own testnet address. If it passes, three council members sign in the Monday round and it is broadcast.

    Everyone votes · council signs
  5. 5

    Say no

    rejected

    A proposal is voted down on purpose. Nothing moves, and the record shows the result.

    Everyone votes
  6. 6

    Change a rule

    rules

    Members vote to change how the DAO works, for example the length of the voting period.

    Two thirds needed
  7. 7

    Lose a key

    replace key

    A member pretends to lose their phone, restores their identity from their printed card on another device, and the DAO carries on.

    One member · council confirms
  8. 8

    Close the DAO

    close

    The last proposal ends the DAO. The council signs one final payment returning all coins to the BTC DAO Testnet Treasury.

    Two thirds needed · council signs
What we ask of members

What's expected of you

  • Vote on every exercise. Each takes a few minutes, and you have three days. Voting no is as useful as voting yes.
  • Vote in public. All votes are visible to everyone, by member name. Use a pseudonym; you never give your legal name.
  • Ask questions in the BTC DAO group. Confusion is the most valuable thing to surface in a practice run.
  • Make at least one proposal. Anyone can propose; you don't need to be on the council.
  • Keep your card. Print or save your identity card when the app offers it. It is how you get back in if you lose your phone.
  • Council only: sign in the Monday round. Open the app on your phone, check the payments and sign. You can do it from anywhere.
  • Stay to the end. Closing the DAO is part of the lesson.
Questions

Before you join

Does it cost anything?

No. Everything runs on Bitcoin's test network. Test coins have no value and go back to the BTC DAO Testnet Treasury when the DAO closes.

Do I need a wallet or any equipment?

Only a phone with a recent browser that supports passkeys. For the payment exercise you also need a testnet address: the wallet guide shows you how to get one in a few minutes.

How do I join or take a council seat?

Post in the BTC DAO group in the Club Orange app. The join rules explain the council seats, the reserve list and how later members join.

How does founding work?

Remotely and at your own pace, within 48 hours. You sign up on the website and post your short code in the group. The founder guide takes you through it in five steps.

Do we ever need to meet or be on a call?

No. Founding, joining and signing are all done remotely, at your own pace, through the website and the BTC DAO group.

Who can see what I do?

Everyone. Proposals, votes and treasury payments are public and permanent. That transparency is how a Bitcoin DAO earns trust without a registered legal entity behind it.

What happens after it closes?

You will know how a Bitcoin DAO works from the inside. Groups that want their own DAO can start one with a name and purpose of their own.

How to join

Join from the BTC DAO group

The BTC DAO group in the Club Orange app is where everything starts. The join rules and the founder guide are pinned there.

  1. Council: post "Council" in the group. The organiser holds one seat; the first four to ask get the others.
  2. Founding members: post "Founder" before the founding deadline and follow the founder guide.
  3. Later members: post "Member" once the DAO is live, and a member will propose you.
