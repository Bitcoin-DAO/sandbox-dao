# BTC DAO · Founder guide

*Pin this in the BTC DAO group after the join rules. For council volunteers and founding members.*

Founding happens remotely and at your own pace, within 48 hours of **Founding open**. No call, no hardware. You need about 10 minutes and your phone.

**Use the same phone and browser from step 1 until you post Confirm.** Until the DAO exists, your key lives only in that browser. Don't clear it, don't switch to private mode, don't change phone.

## 1. Sign up

- Open https://bitcoin-dao.github.io/sandbox-dao on your phone.
- Tap **Founder sign-up**.
- Enter your pseudonym. This name and your votes will be public forever.
- **Council volunteers only:** tick **I am on the council**. Your phone also creates a test treasury key for signing payments. (The app mentions SeedSigner for the real DAO; you don't need one here.)
- Tick the box to accept the charter, then tap **Next**.
- Tap **Create with fingerprint or face** to make your passkey.

## 2. Share your code

- The app shows a QR code and your **short code**.
- Tap **Share** and send the message to this group. It carries your pseudonym, your short code and your full sign-up code. (If your phone can't share, the button copies the message instead: paste it here.)

The organiser pastes your message into the setup page and checks that the short code on screen is exactly the one in your message. Because you posted it yourself, everyone knows the code really belongs to you.

## 3. Wait for the founding

- Leave the app as it is. When all codes are in, the organiser publishes the founding settings and posts **Settings published** here.
- **Council only:** open the app and check the summary: the DAO's name, the five council members, 3 of 5 needed to sign, and the list of founders with their short codes. If it matches what was posted in the group, tap **Approve and sign**. The DAO exists at the third council approval.

## 4. Protect your key and keep your card

- Once the DAO exists, tap **Protect the key and continue** and confirm with your passkey.
- Print or save your **identity card**. It is how you restore your identity on a new phone.
- **Council only:** your test treasury key's 12 words are on your **Profile** page. Write them down on paper and keep them with your card. They're test words, but treat them as practice for the real thing: never photograph them or post them anywhere.

## 5. Post Confirm

- Open the **Members** page and check that your pseudonym and short code are there. Council members also check that they appear on the council.
- Post **Confirm** in this group.
- If anything is wrong, post **Problem** and say what you saw. Don't post Confirm.

The treasury is funded once every founder has posted Confirm. The first exercise follows.
