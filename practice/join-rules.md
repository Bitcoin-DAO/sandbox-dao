# BTC DAO · Sandbox DAO · How to join

*Pin this in the BTC DAO group in the Club Orange app.*

This group runs the Sandbox DAO, a Bitcoin DAO on the test network. No real bitcoin is involved. Everything is public and permanent, including who votes what. Use a pseudonym.

About the Sandbox DAO: https://bitcoin-dao.github.io/practice-dao/about.html

**What you need:** a phone with a recent browser that supports passkeys. Nothing else.

## Council (5 seats, open now)

1. Seats go to the first five people who post **Council** in this group.
2. Anyone who posts Council after the first five goes on the reserve list, in order. If a council volunteer doesn't sign up before the deadline, their seat passes to the next person on the list.
3. If fewer than five people volunteer by the deadline, the group organiser takes the last seat.
4. Council members hold the treasury keys. Any 3 of the 5 must sign before coins move. You sign on your phone, from wherever you are, in the weekly Monday round.

## Founding members (open now)

5. Anyone else who wants to be in from the start posts **Founder** in this group.

## Founding (council and founders)

6. When the organiser posts **Founding open**, follow the founder guide (pinned). You sign up on the website and post your short code here.
7. The deadline is **48 hours** after Founding open. Miss it, and you join later as a member instead.
8. Once the DAO exists, check that you're in it and post **Confirm**. If something looks wrong, post **Problem** and say what you saw.

## Members (after the DAO is live)

9. Post **Member** in this group. Sign up on the website, post your short code here, and a member will propose you. You're voted in over three days.

## For everyone

10. Vote on every exercise. Votes last three days.
11. Keep your identity card when the app offers it. It is how you get back in if you lose your phone.
12. Stay until the DAO is closed. Closing it is part of the practice.
